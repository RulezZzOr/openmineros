Presets
Presets come from Braiins OS autotuner profiles saved on two S19 units (average chip clock per power target). Power figures are estimates (the PSU does not report power): 74 W + 3.54 W/MHz × clock × (V/11.96)². Hashrate scales with the clock; power scales roughly with clock × voltage². Only “eco” has been measured on this machine. Higher points draw much more power and heat — watch temperatures after changing. The clock is ramped in 25 MHz steps and the voltage is raised before the clock.
